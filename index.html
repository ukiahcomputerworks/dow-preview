03 / THE MODEL

Embedded expertise without building another bureaucracy.

Originally founded by Phillip Dow, the firm began with transportation planning and administration. Its role has grown to include fiscal oversight, housing, economic development, project and program implementation, and energy efficiency.

The model is deliberately practical: a compact team becomes the operating capacity rural agencies need to move public priorities from agenda to action.
