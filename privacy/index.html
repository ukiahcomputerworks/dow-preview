Contact-form privacy notice

This notice applies to information submitted through the contact form on this website.

Information collected

The form asks for your name, email address, message, and inquiry type. Organization and telephone information are optional. Do not submit confidential, financial, medical, or other sensitive personal information.

How information is used

The information is used to deliver your inquiry to Dow & Associates and allow the team to respond. It is not collected through this form for advertising or mailing-list enrollment.

Form processing

The form is processed by FormSubmit and delivered by email to Dow & Associates. FormSubmit states that submitted information is processed to provide its service and that its submission archive retains form submissions for 30 days. Review FormSubmit’s privacy terms and service documentation for details.

Other ways to contact the office

You may avoid the online form by calling 707-463-1806 or emailing admin@dow-associates.com.
